# Figure provenance and reuse

These evaluation figures and their reconstructions are separate from the MIT-licensed
runtime. They are excluded from the standalone `.skill` package. Paper authors and the
Matplotlib project do not endorse the reconstructions or their measured fidelity.
Source, scene and PPTX hashes are recorded in [manifest.json](manifest.json).

## CLIP — ICML 2021

Alec Radford et al., *Learning Transferable Visual Models From Natural Language Supervision*.
[Official PMLR proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/radford21a.html).
Input is the authors' `CLIP.png` from `openai/CLIP`, commit
`d05afc436d78f1c48dc0dbf8e5980a9d471f35f6`:
[image](https://github.com/openai/CLIP/blob/d05afc436d78f1c48dc0dbf8e5980a9d471f35f6/CLIP.png),
[MIT license](https://github.com/openai/CLIP/blob/d05afc436d78f1c48dc0dbf8e5980a9d471f35f6/LICENSE).
Copyright (c) 2021 OpenAI. Full terms are retained in [CLIP_MIT.txt](licenses/CLIP_MIT.txt).

Changes by super_img2ppt contributors, 2026-09-07: flattened transparent input onto white;
independently rebuilt labels, subscript text, grids, connectors and four encoder trapezoids
from the bitmap; retained two photographic regions; generated editable PPTX/SVG, previews
and measurements. Source fonts, arrowhead contours and antialiasing are approximations.

## Swin Transformer — ICCV 2021

Ze Liu, Yutong Lin, Yue Cao, Han Hu, Yixuan Wei, Zheng Zhang, Stephen Lin and Baining Guo,
*Swin Transformer: Hierarchical Vision Transformer Using Shifted Windows*.
[Official CVF proceedings](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2021/html/Liu_Swin_Transformer_Hierarchical_Vision_Transformer_Using_Shifted_Windows_ICCV_2021_paper.html).
Input is `figures/teaser.png` from `microsoft/Swin-Transformer`, commit
`f82860bfb5225915aca09c3227159ee9e1df874d`:
[image](https://github.com/microsoft/Swin-Transformer/blob/f82860bfb5225915aca09c3227159ee9e1df874d/figures/teaser.png),
[MIT license](https://github.com/microsoft/Swin-Transformer/blob/f82860bfb5225915aca09c3227159ee9e1df874d/LICENSE).
Copyright (c) Microsoft Corporation. Full terms are retained in [SWIN_MIT.txt](licenses/SWIN_MIT.txt).

Changes by super_img2ppt contributors, 2026-09-07: flattened the transparent bitmap onto white;
reconstructed four panels, 92 text objects, 30 shapes and 375 lines/arrows; retained 34 separate
photo crops with transparent grid strips and redrew their grids as native lines. Mathematical
hats use native line components. Arrow silhouettes, dashed corners, formula sizing and photo
crop antialiasing retain documented differences. No model code or weights were imported.

## Matplotlib heatmap — gallery example, not a conference figure

The Matplotlib development team,
[Creating annotated heatmaps, documentation version 3.11.1](https://matplotlib.org/3.11.1/gallery/images_contours_and_fields/image_annotated_heatmap.html).
Input is the gallery's [second PNG](https://matplotlib.org/3.11.1/_images/sphx_glr_image_annotated_heatmap_002.png).
The associated-documentation license is retained in [MATPLOTLIB_LICENSE.txt](licenses/MATPLOTLIB_LICENSE.txt).

Changes by super_img2ppt contributors, 2026-09-07: read values from the supplied bitmap and
rebuilt 49 cells and their labels as shapes/text; sampled the bitmap colorbar into 16 stops
of one native linear gradient; retained the seven approximately 30-degree top labels as one
acknowledged raster crop. Gallery plotting code/data were not supplied to the evaluator.
These are modified derivatives, not files generated by the original plotting program.

The same PNG URL returned two byte encodings with identical decoded RGB pixels; both inspected
SHA-256 values are fixed in the repository fetcher. The first mismatch and the pixel comparison
remain in `docs/evidence/diverse_cases`. The evaluation used the first recorded byte encoding.

## DDPM — local test only

Jonathan Ho, Ajay Jain and Pieter Abbeel, *Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models*, NeurIPS 2020.
[Official proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2020/hash/4c5bcfec8584af0d967f1ab10179ca4b-Abstract.html),
[author page](https://hojonathanho.github.io/diffusion/).
The author's `assets/img/rate.png`, containing a table and rate-distortion plot, was tested
locally. A general redistribution license for that image was not established. Its source,
derived scene/PPTX/SVG and previews are excluded from this corpus. Engineering measurements,
diagnostics and a fixed-source local retrieval recipe are retained separately.

No font files are distributed or embedded. Required installed fonts and their hashes appear
in each case's `fonts.json`; those hashes do not grant permission to redistribute fonts.
